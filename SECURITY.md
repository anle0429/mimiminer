# Security and rights reports

Contact **work.anle05@gmail.com** for security, copyright or abuse concerns. Include the release version and enough information to reproduce or identify the issue. Do not send Minecraft passwords, account tokens or other people's private information.

For ordinary setup and compatibility reports, use the repository's Issues page. Updates and responses are best-effort; no support or security-fix timeline is promised. Version 0.0.1 is an early release. Obtain binaries only from this repository's Releases page and verify their SHA-256 checksum.
